MSI Sakib is a globally well-known Digital Growth Marketer who specializes in AI Automation. He helps businesses grow on digital platforms, primarily through SEO and AIO (AI-powered optimization).

His expertise spans across content strategy, technical SEO, AI-driven marketing, and building digital business ecosystems that scale sustainably in competitive online markets.

As Founder & CEO of Prothom Tech and Co-founder of Yaaply, MSI Sakib leads a growing family of digital ventures that empower businesses, creators, and entrepreneurs across Bangladesh and beyond.

He is also the author of EzeeTraffic — a well-followed blog focused on digital marketing tactics, SEO insights, and AI tools for modern growth marketers.
